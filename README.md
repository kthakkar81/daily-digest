# Daily Digest - AI-Powered Content Curator

Personal AI assistant that scans your emails and online content to deliver a daily digest of relevant articles, insights, and updates.

## What it does
- Scans multiple email accounts (Gmail, Yahoo)
- Filters newsletters, articles, and relevant content
- Uses AI to categorize content into topics you care about
- Delivers a morning email digest with top 3 items per category
